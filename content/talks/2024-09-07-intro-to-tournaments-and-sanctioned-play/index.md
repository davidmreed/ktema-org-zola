This talk guides non-skating officials (NSOs) on how to move from officiating scrimmages and home mixers to the higher levels of sanctioned play and tournaments.
We cover what makes a sanctioned game sanctioned; what behaviors are important for NSOs; how to get into a tournament; and how to succeed once you're there.

This session expands slightly on a previous session offered in early 2024.

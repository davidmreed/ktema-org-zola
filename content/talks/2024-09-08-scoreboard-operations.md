This talk walks through how to operate the Roller Derby Scoreboard (often called CRG) as a non-skating official.

We set up the scoreboard software. Then we walk through setting up games, running a game, troubleshooting, and downloading paperwork. We talk about the SBO role and how not to be afraid of CRG.

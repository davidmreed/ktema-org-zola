Design processes to be automatable even if you're not ready to automate them.

Person X manually copies information from a text field in System A to System B, making edits along the way based on criteria in a Word doc and their personal judgement? Red flag. You can't automate that.

You need to have
- Well-structured information storage
- in a clear source of truth
- that has an API
to support your process. It'll make Person X's life easier, reduce your bus factor, and lay the groundwork for automating the process and giving Person X something more interesting to do.
